# Official client parity

nai-cli aims to send the same requests as the NovelAI web client, so that the same settings and seed produce the same image. The rules below follow build `adc9438` of the official client.

Request building, image and mask processing and compositing always follow the official client. A few behaviours differ on purpose; they are listed under [Intentional differences](#intentional-differences), where defaults that change the image have a switch that restores the official behaviour.

## Endpoints

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Generation | `POST image.novelai.net/ai/generate-image` returns a zip. The body is `{input, model, action, parameters, use_new_shared_trial: true}` with `action` one of `generate`, `img2img` and `infill`, and `params_version: 4`. Parameter keys are sent in the official order, and whole numbers are written as `JSON.stringify` writes them (`4`, not `4.0`), in every request. When the "stream image generation" setting is on (the default) and the model supports it, the official client instead sends the same body with `stream: "msgpack"` to `/ai/generate-image-stream` and reads previews and the final images from a msgpack stream, falling back to `/ai/generate-image` after repeated failures. Its requests also carry `x-correlation-id` and `x-initiated-at` headers. |
| Tokenizers | `GET novelai.net/tokenizer/compressed/NAME?v=2&static=true`, a raw deflate of JSON: `qwen35_tokenizer.def` for V5 and `t5_tokenizer.def` for V4.5 and V4. Qwen3.5 is byte-level BPE: NFC, special tokens split off first, the rest split by the file's pattern. T5 is Unigram: brackets and `N::` weights removed, split on whitespace, `▁` prefixed, the best path by Viterbi with unknown characters scored 10 below the lowest piece, and one `</s>` per text. nai-cli's port returns the same ids as the official code on 3,000 generated prompts. |
| Account | `GET image.novelai.net/user/subscription`. |
| Login | `POST image.novelai.net/user/login` with `{key}` returns `accessToken`, valid for 30 days. The key is Argon2id (2 passes, 1953 KiB, 1 lane, 64 bytes) of the password, salted with the 16-byte BLAKE2b of the first 6 UTF-16 units of the password, the lowercased email and `novelai_data_access_key`, written as unpadded base64url and cut to 64 characters. |
| Director tools | Multipart `POST /ai/augment-image` with `request` set to `{req_type, use_new_shared_trial: true, prompt?, defry?, width, height, image}`. Only `colorize` and `emotion` take `prompt` and `defry` (0 to 5). For `emotion`, `prompt` is `EMOTION;;DESCRIPTION`. There are 24 emotions. |
| Pixel snapping | Runs in the browser and sends nothing. Pitches between 3 and 24 are found from the periodicity of the colour changes between columns and rows, chosen coarse to fine by the colour spread inside the cells, then each axis is fitted on its own; a half or a third of the pitch replaces it when it lowers the mean error by more than 20%, unless over-refining is avoided. Cell lines follow the edges, cells take the median colour inside a 27% margin, and the palette is an octree of up to 256 colours merged in Lab: up to a distance of 6.5 for auto, down to the count given otherwise (1 to 256; the slider starts at 16, but a smaller number can be typed). Images above 4000000 pixels are shrunk first. `--upscale` scales the cells by the rounded mean of the source's width and height per cell, which differs from the pitches when a large image was shrunk first. |
| Upscale | Multipart `POST /ai/upscale` with `{image, model: nai-diffusion-5-curated, declared_blur_sigma: 0}`. The image is not resized, and images over 3145728 pixels in area are refused. |
| Tag suggestions | `GET /ai/generate-image/suggest-tags?model=MODEL&prompt=QUERY`, encoded with `encodeURIComponent`. V5 adds `type=animev5`, or `furryv5` in furry mode; V4.5 in furry mode asks with `model=nai-diffusion-furry-3` instead. `lang=jp` follows when more than 30% of the query, counted in UTF-16 code units, is CJK punctuation, kana, half- or full-width forms or Han characters. The query is trimmed and the pattern `[/[{}]]` is removed before sending. Japanese results are a bare list of `{jp_tag, en_tag, power}`; others are `{tags: [{tag, count, confidence}]}`. |
| Vibe encoding | Multipart `POST /ai/encode-vibe` with `{image, information_extracted, mask, model}`, where `mask` is left out when unset. A mask is a PNG part of its own, or names the image's part when it is the same; an empty mask stays in the request with no part. Stored encodings are looked up by the mask as stored, empty or wrapped over lines. New Vibes default to strength 0.6, and to information extracted 0.7 for V4.5 Full and 1 otherwise. With several Vibes, `normalize_reference_strength_multiple` is on by default and scales strengths whose absolute values sum above 1 down to a sum of 1 before sending; turned off, they are sent as given. |
| Model detection | Images are attributed by the hash in `Source`: `657484A5` and `0ADF9AB7` are V5 Full, `DB276663` is V5 Curated, `93F4BD30` and `70AB5786` are V5 Medium. V4 Full is `37442FCA`, `4F49EC75`, `CA4B7203`, `79F47848` and `F6302A9D`, and V4 Curated `7ABFFA2A`, `C1CCBA86` and `770A9E12`, while V4 images with `5AB81C7C` or `B5A2A797` are V4.5 Curated. Other V5 hashes count as V5 Curated, other V4.5 hashes as V4.5 Curated and other V4 hashes as V4 Curated. |

| Item | V5 Full, Curated | V5 Medium | V4.5 Full, Curated |

| Token limit | Full 1471, Curated 703 | 1471 | 512 |
| Tokenizer | Qwen3.5 | Same | T5 |
| Rendered text, by the documentation | Full 750, Curated 374 characters | Not stated | 118 characters |
| Characters | Up to 32, freely placed | Same | Up to 6, snapped to a 5×5 grid |
| Furry mode | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Noise schedule | Not selectable; removed and re-added as `karras` at the end. With `v5_hidden_options`, selectable in the same place | Not selectable | Selectable, without `native`; a sampler picked without a schedule chosen for it gets `exponential` for DPM++ 2M and `karras` otherwise. The old samplers `ddim` and `ddim_v3` from imported settings are sent as `k_euler_ancestral` |
| Transparency, automatic text | Yes; `straight_alpha` follows the "straight alpha" setting, on by default | Yes | No |
| Vibes, Variety+ | No; Variety+ with `v5_hidden_options`, at sigma 58 | No | Yes |
| Precise references | Yes, but not when inpainting. No fidelity: every reference is sent at fidelity 1, a secondary strength of 0. Outfit references and the same-outfit preference | With a precise reference, V5 Full runs instead, as in the official client | Yes, also when inpainting. Fidelity; no outfit references, and the same-outfit kinds are sent as `character` and `character&style` |
| Inpainting model | Full: `nai-diffusion-5-full-inpainting`. **Curated: `nai-diffusion-4-5-curated-inpainting`** | `nai-diffusion-5-full-medium-inpainting` | The matching `-inpainting` model |
| Limits | Steps 1 to 50, CFG from 0 with no upper limit, CFG rescale 0 to 1, image-to-image strength 0.01 to 0.99 and noise 0 to 0.99, inpainting strength 0.01 to 1, information extracted 0.01 to 1, seeds up to 10 digits. Vibe strengths and precise reference strength and fidelity have no limit. Up to 16 precise references, of kind character and style by default (`character&style`, which on V5 keeps the outfit). Images per request by area: 4 up to 3145728 pixels, rising to 8 at 245760 or below. Samplers are the V4 and V5 list: Euler Ancestral, Euler, DPM++ 2S Ancestral, DPM++ 2M SDE, DPM++ 2M and DPM++ SDE, without DDIM | | |

V4 Full (`nai-diffusion-4-full`) and V4 Curated (`nai-diffusion-4-curated-preview`), which the official client marks as no longer recommended, follow V4.5 except: default CFG 5.5, no precise references, no Enhance hint, no human or furry UC preset, a Variety+ sigma of 19 instead of 58 before scaling, and a legacy UC switch that sets `legacy_uc` in the parameters and in `v4_negative_prompt`. Their quality tags are `no text, best quality, very aesthetic, absurdres` and `rating:general, best quality, very aesthetic, absurdres`. They inpaint with `nai-diffusion-4-full-inpainting` and `nai-diffusion-4-curated-inpainting`.

Inpainting applies every rule of the inpainting model: quality tags, UC presets, features and pricing. Vibes and precise references are the exception: the official client offers and prices them by the model chosen, and no V5 model takes precise references when inpainting, as its inpainting panel says. Vibes are never sent when inpainting. See Intentional differences for how nai-cli treats a precise reference there. V5 Curated inpainting therefore uses the V4.5 Curated quality tags and UC presets, has no 1.5× price factor and sends `noise_schedule` where V4.5 does. A UC preset the inpainting model lacks is replaced in the official fallback order (furry, heavy, light, none); a missing quality tier becomes standard.

## Prompt text

Quality tags are added to the prompt first, then the Enhance hint and the furry prefix. The negative prompt is computed from that result, and the `teXt:` block is added last.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Furry mode | `fur dataset, ` is prepended after the quality tags and the Enhance hint, unless the text starts with `fur dataset` or `background dataset`. An empty prompt becomes `fur dataset, very aesthetic, ...`, and `text: HELLO` becomes `fur dataset, very aesthetic, masterpiece, no texttext: HELLO`. Only models with furry mode. |
| Quality tags | V5 standard `very aesthetic, masterpiece, no text`, light `very aesthetic, amazing quality, no text`; V4.5 Full `very aesthetic, masterpiece, no text`; V4.5 Curated `very aesthetic, masterpiece, no text, -0.8::feet::, rating:general`. |
| Placement | Only in the first mix segment (separated by `\|`). If the segment contains `text:` (any case), the tags go before the first one, and every `text:` marker in the segment takes the first one's spelling. The result is `PROMPT, TAGS`, or just the tags for an empty prompt. Whitespace and trailing commas are kept, so `wind,` becomes `wind,, very aesthetic, ...`. |
| Leading `text:` | A prompt starting with `text:` becomes `very aesthetic, masterpiece, no texttext: ...`. nai-cli does the same, and `lint` reports `text_marker_prefix`. |
| Transparency | With V5 `--transparent`, `transparent background, ` precedes the quality tags and `tag_hint_transparent_background` is sent. |
| UC presets | The negative prompt is split on `\|` and the preset is prepended to the first segment as `PRESET, SEGMENT`, or alone for an empty segment. The user's text is never changed. |
| nsfw | For non-Curated models with a UC preset, `nsfw, ` is prepended to the negative prompt when the lowercased prompt, after quality tags and before the `teXt:` block, does not contain `nsfw`. |
| Verbatim negative prompt | The official "skip UC processing": no preset, no nsfw, and no `tag_hint_uc_preset` except on V5 Medium. In nai-cli this is `--neg-verbatim`. |
| Automatic text | V5 copies quoted text from the prompt and the characters into a trailing `teXt: ...` block, reversed when mostly CJK and in reading order when positions are used. Nothing is added if the prompt already contains `text:`. |
| Characters | `char_captions` entries are `{char_caption, centers}` and `characterPrompts` entries are `{prompt, uc, center, enabled}`. V4.5 snaps `char_captions` to the grid but keeps the original coordinates in `characterPrompts`. Without positions, characters take the first free slot of 25 in the official order, starting (0.5, 0.5), (0.3, 0.5), (0.7, 0.5). A slot is taken within a distance of 0.1 with free placement, or by grid cell otherwise. Characters with an empty prompt take a slot and are then dropped, so the others keep their places; they still count toward the limit. |
| Character negative prompt | A character added on V4 Full or V4 Curated starts with `lowres, aliasing, ` as its negative prompt; on other models it starts empty. A `--char` without `--char-neg` gets the same, and `gen_args` writes every character's negative once one differs from what the model fills in. |
| Random prompt | Models with character prompts use one recipe: 1, 2, 3 or no people weighted 70, 20, 7 and 5, each a girl or boy at 60 to 30, then weighted picks from 39 word lists with fixed odds per step. An entry is `[text, weight, flags it sets, flags it needs, flags that rule it out]`. Characters get `lowres, aliasing, `, the centre 0,0, and replace those in the editor. |
| Character `1girl` | When any character prompt contains `1girl` or `1boy`, the first of each in every character prompt becomes `girl` and `boy`, and the edited prompts are kept. Enhance and Variations do not do this. |
| Numeric weights | Each `::` sets the weight to the number right before it (`-?\d*\.?\d*$`), where a bare `-` or `.` means 0 and no number means 1. A number before a closing `::` thus starts a new weight. The official editor warns about weights above 70, `1.5 ::` (space before `::`) and `::1.5` (number after). `nai lint` uses the same parsing for `weight_numeric_close`, `weight_spaced`, `weight_number_after`, `weight_unclosed` and `weight_stray_close`. |
| Enhance | Except at `max`, `, -2::upscaled, blurry::,` is added after the quality tags, before the first `text:` or at the end, unless the prompt already contains `upscaled, blurry`. |
| Token counts | Each prompt box expands macros, replaces every `\|\|a\|b\|\|` group by its longest option (the later one on a tie), splits the text into mix segments and encodes each segment. The first segment of the prompt gets the quality tags and the first segment of the negative prompt the UC preset and nsfw rule; character prompts are counted as typed. The counts of the base prompt and its characters are added up and compared with the token limit; above it the client warns that the rest is cut off. |

## Images and masks

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Resizing | Images use Pica's Lanczos3 (14-bit fixed point, premultiplied when there is alpha); masks use nearest neighbour at pixel centres. `resample.py` reproduces the integer arithmetic and matches the official bundle byte for byte. |
| Base image | Stretched to the generation size. Models without transparency get a white background, blended by alpha with colours rounded half to even. |
| Import size | Rotated to portrait, the current size is used if the aspect ratio matches and it fits; else the image's own size if both sides are multiples of 64 within 3145728 pixels; else a size scaled from a 1216 long side or an 896 short side (rounded to 64), whichever ratio is closer; else 512×512. |
| Inpainting canvas | The editor scales images with a side above 2560 down (flooring the other side), rounds each side up to a multiple of 64 and stretches the image to fit. On closing, a canvas within 3145728 pixels becomes the generation size; a larger one follows the import rule. |
| Precise reference, V4.5 | After the stealth cleanup, the image is fitted into whichever of 1024×1536, 1536×1024 and 1472×1472 is closest in aspect ratio (the first on a tie): one side fills the canvas and the other is rounded with `Math.round`. It is resized with Lanczos3 if the size changes, placed on a transparent canvas at rounded centre offsets and given a black background, as V4.5 does not keep transparency. Matches the official code byte for byte. |
| Precise reference, V5 | After the stealth cleanup, the image goes to whichever of 97 size buckets, from 256×4096 through 1024×1024 to 4096×256, is closest in log aspect ratio (the first on a tie). It is resized with Lanczos3 to cover the bucket, each side `floor(side × scale + 0.5)` and at least the bucket's, and cropped from the centre at floored offsets. An image already the bucket's size is sent as it is. Transparency is kept. The pixels match the official code. |
| Stealth cleanup | If the base image's alpha carries `stealth_pngcomp` data and its area is at most 16777216, alpha 254 becomes 255 and 1 becomes 0. |
| Mask | Stored at 1/8 of the canvas: nearest-neighbour scaled to 1/8 of the generation size, thresholded at alpha above 155, then scaled back to the generation size on black (white repaints). nai-cli reads opaque masks by luminance. Empty masks are rejected before sending, because NovelAI charges for them. |
| Inpainting parameters | `add_original_image: false`; compositing is done by the client. |
| Focus | A crop (in latent units, ×8 for pixels) under 0.8 × 1048576 pixels is scaled up within 1048576 and floored to multiples of 64; larger crops keep their size. The crop is resized with Lanczos3 and its mask with nearest neighbour. Character centres map to `(x × canvas width − crop left) / crop width`, clamped to 0-1. The official client requires drawing the crop by hand; nai-cli chooses it automatically (see below). |
| Compositing | Always applied; without focus the box is the whole image and the base is the image sent. The feather mask dilates the 1/8 mask by 4, upscales it 8× with nearest neighbour and box-blurs it twice with radius 20 (multiply by 39, shift right 16). The result's alpha is multiplied by the feather (`Math.round`) and resized to the box with Lanczos3; the base's alpha inside the box is multiplied by `(255 − feather) / 255`, and the two are blended as `colour = (result·a + base·b) / min(1, a + b)`, alpha `round(255·min(1, a + b))`. The result's alpha LSBs are copied column-major, followed by the text chunks. Verified byte for byte against the official worker. |
| Canvas expansion | The editor's enlarged canvas is transparent and gets the same white background before sending. `--expand` does the same and adds the new area to the mask, with one latent cell (8 pixels) into the source along each expanded side so the seam is repainted; each side is rounded up to a multiple of 64, as the resize dialog rounds it, so the source stays on the latent grid. |
| Image to image | `image`, `strength`, `noise`, `color_correct: false`; `extra_noise_seed = seed - 1` whenever a base image is sent. |
| Size rounding | The area is checked against 3145728 pixels first, then the base image is resized and the sizes are rounded to the nearest multiple of 64 (ties up), so the base image can differ from the requested size. |
| Enhance | Levels 1 to 5: strength 0.2, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, with noise 0.1 at level 5. Scales: 832×1216 offers 1.5 and 1; other sizes offer 2, 1.5 and 1 where the result is within 3145728 pixels in multiples of 64. V5 images under 0.8 × 3145728 also offer `max`, sent at the original size with `upscaled_enhance: true` and rounded to multiples of 64 like every request. Otherwise the base image is resized to `floor(side × scale)` before size rounding, so 1.5× on 832×1216 sends a 1248×1824 image with 1280×1856 parameters. One image, new seed. `max` comes back scaled by √(3145728 / area) on each side, 1467×2144 for 832×1216; nai-cli prices it with each side rounded half up. |
| Variations | Strength 0.8, noise 0.1, new seed, 3 images or the size's limit if lower, using the image's own prompts. |
| Director tool input | Scaled down above 3145728 − 2000 pixels, or up to about 1048576 below 1011712, flooring each side. |

## Pricing

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Generation | Per image `ceil(2.951823174884865e-6 × pixels + 5.753298233447344e-7 × pixels × steps)`. Medium always uses 23 steps divided by 1.75, without rounding; V5 models multiply by 1.5. The result is multiplied by strength, rounded up, and at least 2. Images above 140 are not allowed. |
| Strength | Image to image uses `strength`; inpainting uses `inpaintImg2ImgStrength`. |
| Opus free tier | One image per request, up to 1048576 pixels and 28 steps; precise references are charged on top. V5 draws on a free quota and is charged normally once it runs out. Opus means tier 3 or above on a subscription whose `expiresAt` has not passed; business, service, support and admin accounts count whatever the expiry. The reply's own `active` flag is not used, so a cancelled renewal stays Opus until it expires. |
| References | 5 per precise reference per image. Vibes are sent and charged only without precise references and outside inpainting: 2 per Vibe beyond 4, plus 2 per Vibe that needs encoding. |
| Director tools | Scaled into 3145728 pixels, then up to 1048576, and priced as one 28-step `nai-diffusion-3` image with the area formula: `max(ceil(2.951823174884865e-6 × pixels + 5.753298233447344e-7 × pixels × 28), 2)`. Background removal costs `3 × price + 5` and returns three images, saved as masked, generated and blend. Free with Opus up to 1048576 pixels, except background removal. |
| Upscale | By the source's area: 1 Anlas up to 1048576 pixels, 2 up to 1747627, 3 up to 2446678 and 4 up to 3145728. Opus pays the same. |
| Enhance at `max` | Priced at the server's output size: sides rounded to 16 and doubled, scaled into 3145728 pixels and rounded to 32 (floored if still too large). 832×1216 is priced as 1440×2144. |
| Actual spend | The balance difference before and after the request, both read while holding the generation lock. The estimate decides whether to ask for confirmation and is priced against the balance read under the lock. |

## Reading images

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Settings | The `Comment` PNG text chunk (tEXt, iTXt or zTXt); else the EXIF `UserComment`, where NovelAI's WebP images keep them, after an 8-byte charset header (`UNICODE` is UTF-16, little-endian only with a byte order mark) with trailing NULs removed; else the stealth data in the alpha LSBs, read column-major as `stealth_pnginfo` or gzip-compressed `stealth_pngcomp`, skipped above 16777216 pixels or when the stated length exceeds the image. The official import reads tEXt and uncompressed iTXt only, and of the stealth data only `stealth_pngcomp`; nai-cli also reads zTXt, compressed iTXt and `stealth_pnginfo`, which other tools write. |
| Negative prompt | A leading `nsfw` and its `, ` are removed, then the preset named by `tag_hint_uc_preset`, or else the longest preset that matches the start of the first segment. nai-cli tries both nsfw rules, keeps the one that rebuilds the text exactly, and records it in `gen_args` as `--official-nsfw` or `--no-official-nsfw`. |
| Prompt | The `teXt:` block and quality tags are removed, using `tag_hint_qt` when present and trying each tier otherwise. With `tag_hint_transparent_background`, `transparent background, ` before the tags is tried first, in the official import's order. As in the official import, a leading `fur dataset, ` is removed and turns on furry mode for models that have it. |
| Variety+ | On whenever `skip_cfg_above_sigma` is present. Its value follows from the size, so it is not read. |
| Vibe normalization | Guessed as in the official import from two or more strengths: a sum of 0.99 to 1.01 means on, above 1 means off, and otherwise unknown. Off is recorded in `gen_args` as `--no-normalize-vibe-strength`. |
| Size | Rerunning aligns each side to the nearest multiple of 64, ties upward, as the official client does before sending. See Intentional differences for sizes that then exceed the pixel limit. |
| Stored Vibes | The official import restores the encodings in `reference_image_multiple` as Vibes without images for the image's model, with `reference_strength_multiple` and `reference_information_extracted_multiple` or the defaults. `gen_args` brings them back with `--vibes-from`. Precise references are not kept in images. |
| Alpha mode | `straight_alpha` is recorded in `gen_args` as `--premultiplied-alpha` or `--no-premultiplied-alpha`. |
| Vibe files | A `.naiv4vibe` holds one Vibe; a `.naiv4vibebundle` is `{identifier: "novelai-vibe-transfer-bundle", version: 1, vibes}`. PNG images exported by the official client carry either as base64 JSON in a `naidata` text chunk. `importInfo` gives the strength, information extracted and an optional `mask`; a missing or null value takes the default, and malformed `encodings` fail the import. Encodings are stored under the SHA-256 of the parameters sorted by name, without unset ones, such as `information_extracted:0.7,mask:...`. A Vibe's `id` is the SHA-256 of its image, or of its first encoding when its type is `encoding`; the import refuses a mismatch, an id that is not 64 lowercase hex digits and a thumbnail that is not a base64 data URL. nai-cli also rejects encoding text with a NUL or an unpaired surrogate before using it in a request. A Vibe without its image keeps its stored information extracted. |
| Import | `--from-image` follows the import dialog for an image from disk: prompt, UC and characters are on by default; settings, seed and Vibes are off. The quality toggle goes with settings and the UC preset with UC. Settings leave legacy UC as the editor has it and the alpha mode to its setting, as `doImportSettings` does; Enhance and Variations, which redo the image, keep the image's, and `gen_args` records both. Importing settings or Vibes switches to the image's model; on another model, each value is kept only if the model has it. A value of 0 is imported like any other, as the official import assigns every key the image has; one the image lacks or holds out of range is left to the command line and the config. Clean Brackets removes `[]{}` from every prompt and then spaces the commas (`cleanBrackets:y`, `replace(/[[\]{}]/g,"")`). Actual Prompts takes `actual_prompts` from the settings, the options drawn from `||a|b||` groups. |
| Drawn groups | Enhance, Variations and inpainting an image replace each `\|\|a\|b\|\|` group with the option the image drew, found by matching its stored prompt against `actual_prompts`; groups the image did not have, or a stored prompt that does not explain what was sent, stay as they are. |
| Reruns | Whatever would not rebuild exactly is kept verbatim: `--neg-verbatim` for the negative prompt, and quality tags and automatic text turned off for the prompt. |

`tests/test_roundtrip.py` runs every private sample through `nai meta`, `gen_args` and `nai gen --dry-run` and requires both prompts to match the recorded ones exactly.

## Grids

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Values | Numbers are separated by commas, as values or ranges `START-END:STEP` with a step of 1 by default: the count is `floor((END-START)/STEP + 1e-9) + 1`, integers are rounded half up and decimals kept to 12 significant digits. A range of more than 40 values, a step of 0 or less and an end below the start are refused. Seeds also take `r` or `rN`. Text takes one value per line, blank lines dropped. |
| Axes | Parameters on one axis step together, a shorter list repeating its last value; a cell's label joins its values with `, ` and is cut to 39 characters and `…`. A parameter may not be given twice on one axis or on both. |
| Cells | Row by row, at most 40, one image each. The official grid also varies `uncond_scale`, `sm` and `sm_dyn`; nai-cli does not send the first and has no V3 models for the others. The official client shows the grid only in development builds. |

## Writing files

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Vibe export | Fields are written in the order `identifier, version, type, image, id, encodings, name, thumbnail, createdAt, importInfo`, others are dropped, and the JSON is indented by two spaces. A new image Vibe gets a 256-pixel JPEG thumbnail at quality 0.8 with transparency on black and `createdAt` in milliseconds. Exporting without images keeps, for each image Vibe, the encoding for the model and settings in use under `unknown`, with that encoding's SHA-256 as the id. A bundle is named `YYYY-MM-DD.naiv4vibebundle` by the UTC date; written into a PNG, it goes into an uncompressed `naidata` iTXt chunk right after IHDR as base64 of the UTF-8 JSON, named `NAME.naiv4vibebundle.png` after the image. |
| File names | The template setting, off by default, takes the tokens and filters listed in [Usage](usage.md#configuration). `{prompt}` is cut to 80 characters unless `first`, `last`, `words` or `tags` is given; `{characters}` names up to 3 characters joined by `+`, each by its name, else its first tag, or its second when the first is `girl`, `female`, `boy`, `male`, `1girl` or `1boy`. The result has `\n\r"%*/:<>?\\\|` replaced by `_`, is trimmed and stops at 180 characters, or sooner where it would pass the 255 bytes Linux and macOS take for a name, a label such as `_redacted` kept whole; an empty one falls back to the first 80 characters of the prompt and ` s-SEED`. Generation passes the typed prompt, the seed plus the image's index, the size of the image returned, and the label `image2image`, `inpaint`, `enhanced` or `variations`. Director tool and upscale results take the source image's prompt, seed, model and characters, the tool's name (`emotion-EMOTION` for emotion) and the result's size, and are named by the template only when it has a `{type}` token; otherwise, and with the template off, they are named `SOURCE_TOOL`, `SOURCE_emotion_EMOTION` for emotion, with `_masked`, `_generated` and `_blend` for background removal. The official parser knows 13 tokens: `prompt`, `seed`, `date`, `time`, `model`, `modelname`, `resolution`, `width`, `height`, `index`, `id`, `type` and `characters`. |

## Intentional differences

| Item | Official client | nai-cli | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| V5 defaults | CFG 7, 23 steps | CFG 4, 28 steps; configurable in `[defaults]` | The settings most commonly used with V5; 28 steps remains free with Opus. |
| V5 Full effort | Medium by default | High by default; `effort` in `[defaults]` or `--effort` picks medium. A model chosen as `v5-medium`, or taken from an image, keeps its effort | High is the full model, and the one the defaults above were chosen for. |
| nsfw | Added to the negative prompt unless the prompt contains it | Never added; removed from the negative prompt when the prompt contains it. `official_nsfw` restores the official rule | Avoids an nsfw prompt being cancelled by the default negative prompt. |
| Hidden V5 controls | Variety+ and noise schedule not selectable | `v5_hidden_options` enables them on Full and Curated; import and rerun preserve them when enabled. Inpainting follows the model that runs. | For trying them by hand; off by default. |
| Image format | Requests WebP and converts to PNG | Requests PNG | One conversion fewer; the settings are stored either way. |
| Generation endpoint | Streams from `/ai/generate-image-stream` by default, with correlation headers | `/ai/generate-image`, the official fallback, without those headers | The images and settings are the same; a command line has no previews to show. |
| Token counts | The prompt box counts the typed prompt with quality tags | The text as sent, which also includes the Enhance hint and the `teXt:` block | Counts what NovelAI receives. nai-cli has no macros to expand. |
| Out-of-range values | Clamped | Rejected with `bad_args` | Silent changes are hard to notice on the command line. |
| Resending requests | Only after a 400 `INVALID_CACHE_KEYS` reply | Waits and resends after 429, and once after a failed connection. Free requests are also resent once after a dropped connection or a 5xx; paid ones are not, and the error says `maybe_processed` | 429 and a failed connection mean nothing ran. A paid request is never sent twice when the first may have been charged. |
| Focus | Drawn by hand | Automatic when the crop covers the mask, fits in 1 MP and is smaller than the canvas; `--focus` and `--no-focus` override | There is nothing to draw on in a terminal. Small repairs stay free and large images keep their size. |
| Canvas stretching | The browser's `drawImage` | Pica Lanczos3 | Browser scaling cannot be reproduced exactly. Only images not already a multiple of 64 or above 2560 are affected. |
| Text chunks after compositing | Rewritten as iTXt, decoding tEXt as Latin-1 | Copied byte for byte | Same content, without corrupting non-Latin text. |
| Settings for Enhance and Variations | Taken from the editor | Taken from the image and overridden by the options given, empty values and `--no-` switches included; `--prompt` is required when the image has none. The image's prompts are sent as stored unless a tidying switch is given | A command line has no editor state. |
| Prompt tidying | None | `--format-prompt` and `--space-numeric-close`, off by default | Writing convenience. Reruns from `gen_args` turn them off. |
| Tag lookups | Only for queries of 2 to 20 characters | Any query that is not empty | Checking a long tag such as `hand on another's shoulder` needs the whole name. The server answers either way. |
| Opus free tier with precise references | The price shown counts the image as not free when a precise reference is used (`tw`: `!W.characterRef`) | The image stays free and only the references are counted | NovelAI charges 5 for each reference, of any type, and nothing for the image. |
| Importing characters | The import dialog can append the image's characters to those already in the editor | Characters given on the command line replace the image's; Vibes given with `--vibe` are added to the image's | A command line starts empty, so there is nothing to append to except what is given in the same command. |
| Seed when rerunning an image | The import dialog brings in the prompts, characters, settings and Vibes but leaves the seed out by default | `gen_args` includes an in-range seed; it leaves out numeric settings outside the request limits and reports them in `warnings`. Enhance and Variations still take a new seed | `gen_args` is meant to reproduce the image; a later `--seed` replaces it. |
| Invalid Vibes in a bundle | Dropped from the bundle | The whole file is refused with `bad_args` | A Vibe missing from the request would go unnoticed. |
| Rerun sizes above the pixel limit | An imported 1467×2144 image is sent at 1472×2176, which exceeds the limit, so the request asks for no images | The longer side is reduced the way the size box clamps it (1472×2112), and `warnings` says so | The official request cannot produce an image. |
| File names | `PROMPT s-SEED` unless the template setting is on; an invalid template falls back to that name | `HHMMSS_SEED` in a folder per day unless `[output] filename` is set, and folders by `[output] folder`; an invalid template or folder rule is refused with `config_missing` before sending, control characters also become `_`, and a name or folder the file system refuses falls back to the default name under the output folder with a warning; the template also takes `year`, `month`, `day`, `steps`, `cfg` and `sampler`, which the official parser refuses | Short names that sort by time and folders chosen in the config; nothing paid for is lost to a bad name. |
| Local models | None | `nai interrogate` reads tags with an ONNX tagger, and `object:` regions mask the object under a point with EfficientViT-SAM, both on this computer after an optional install | Free tag reading and object masks; nothing is downloaded unless asked |
| ComfyUI prompts | Not supported; a lone `\|` divides the prompt as written | `--comfyui-characters`, off by default, splits COUPLE, AND with MASK or AREA, and lines starting with `\|` into characters placed at their regions' centres | Prompts written for ComfyUI carry over without retyping each character |
| Random prompt flags | A pick adds back the flags its entry needed, so no flag is ever set and entries needing one never come up | The flags an entry declares are set, each character starts with its gender's flag, legless bodies drop the legs and feet flags, uncovered feet are flagged barefoot, and five records are corrected; `--random-prompt=official` keeps the official code | Without the flags, female-only clothes, legwear and shoes never appear. |
| Grid seeds | A cell without a seed in the editor or on an axis draws its own | One random seed for the whole grid when neither the command nor an axis sets one and no image is imported | Cells then differ only along the axes, which is what a comparison is for. |
| Grid sizes and results | Width and height are separate parameters; the cells are shown on the canvas with their labels | `size` takes `WxH` names; a labelled sheet is saved beside the first image | A command line has no canvas to show them on, and a size is one option there. |
| Vibe names | The first and last 6 characters of the id | The source file's name | Easier to recognise in the official client after importing. |
| Precise reference when inpainting with V5 | The panel says the reference is ignored and the price leaves it out. V5 Full and Medium leave it out of the request; V5 Curated still sends it, since the request checks the V4.5 Curated inpainting model | Refused with `model_unsupported` | A reference given on the command line was asked for; generating without it, or sending one the price left out, would both go unnoticed. |
| Outfit references on V4.5 | Not offered; one kept from V5 is skipped with a notice | Refused with `model_unsupported` (`feature: outfit_reference`) | A reference given on the command line was asked for; dropping it changes the image. |

## Other notes

- `uncond_scale` is never sent; the official client only strips it from old saved settings.
- When switching models, a quality tier the new model lacks becomes standard.
- Settings an image keeps under the `custom` model of V5's test site are read as V5 Curated, whose weights the site ran.
- `v5_hidden_options` sends a noise schedule and Variety+ on V5 Full and V5 Curated. The server takes them and they change the image, but the official client does not offer them, so NovelAI may change or drop them.
- Pixel snapping follows the browser's code, computed in double precision where the browser uses single precision on the GPU, so near ties can fall the other way.
- V5 takes up to 32 characters, as the official client allows; NovelAI's documentation says 22.
- Rendered text limits come from NovelAI's documentation, which calls them tokens on its model page and characters, spaces and newlines included, on its text rendering page. nai-cli counts characters and only warns.
- Vibe masks are carried over from imported files and sent as the official client sends them; the official client has no way to draw one.
